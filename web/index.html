Known limits.

  1. Old binary .doc files are not read. Save them as .docx.
  2. Private or login-only videos are out of scope.
  3. A refresh clears the on-screen list. Upload the document again. Finished files are still skipped.
  4. FFmpeg has to be installed separately. Without it, video and audio cannot be merged.
